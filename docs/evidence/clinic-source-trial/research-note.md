# Clinic Follow-Up Continuity: Buyer Hypothesis & Research Plan

**Purpose:** source material for a buyer-hypothesis exercise, not a product-performance claim.

## Hypothesis / 假设

**Hypothesis (unverified):** Clinic staff may lose follow-up continuity between an initial inquiry, booking, and visit. The potentially useful buyer problem is not “more automation” by default; it is whether a clearly owned, reviewable follow-up workflow fits existing clinic work without confusing responsibility or clinical escalation. Test ownership, workflow fit, and escalation needs before proposing a pilot.

**假设（尚未验证）：** 诊所员工可能在初次咨询、预约和到访之间失去跟进连续性。潜在价值并非默认“增加自动化”，而是确认责任明确、可供员工复核的跟进流程是否适合诊所现有工作，同时不混淆职责或临床升级路径。提出试点前，应先验证责任归属、流程适配和升级需求。

## Evidence and sources / 证据与来源

**Available evidence:** only the approved brief's synthetic figures: 86 inquiries, 62 booked, 41 visited, and USD 16400 associated revenue during the same synthetic reporting period. These figures are synthetic exercise inputs, not observations of a real clinic. The brief supplies no source records, definitions, attribution method, or independent validation. The arithmetic differences between stages do not establish why people did not advance, whether follow-up was missed, or whether revenue was caused by any workflow.

**证据边界：** 仅有获准简报中的合成数字：同一合成报告期间有 86 次咨询、62 次预约、41 次到访及 USD 16400 相关收入。这些数字只是练习输入，并非真实诊所观察。简报没有提供原始记录、指标定义、归因方法或独立验证。阶段间的数量差异不能说明未继续的原因、是否漏掉跟进，也不能证明任何流程带来了收入。

**Evidence unavailable:** real clinic records; staff or buyer interviews; patient-level information; baseline workflow; response times; follow-up ownership; reasons for booking or not booking; visit and revenue definitions; revenue attribution; willingness to pay; competitive or market evidence; clinical, operational, or financial outcomes. No external sources were consulted or authorized. Do not cite or imply any.

**证据不可用：** 真实诊所记录、员工或买家访谈、患者级信息、现行流程基线、响应时间、跟进责任、预约与未预约原因、到访及收入定义、收入归因、支付意愿、市场或竞品证据，以及任何临床、运营或财务结果。未查询或获准使用外部来源；不得引用或暗示存在此类来源。

## Buyer questions / 买家问题

1. Walk me through the last inquiry that did not reach a visit. What happened, and how do you know?
2. Who owns the next step at inquiry, booking, cancellation/no-show, and post-visit stages? Where does ownership change or become unclear?
3. What tools and handoffs are used today? Which step, if any, creates avoidable work or lost continuity?
4. What must staff review or approve before any message or action? What must never be automated?
5. Which situations require clinical escalation, who receives it, and what is the existing safe route? The exercise must not invent clinical advice or replace clinic policy.
6. What would make a proposed workflow unacceptable: extra clicks, duplicate records, timing, privacy, tone, liability, or something else?
7. Before showing a price or suggesting a budget, ask unaided: “What would you pay for this, if anything? Please give a currency and amount. Zero is a valid answer.” Follow up only to understand the reasoning and buying unit.

## Concrete research plan / 具体研究计划

- **Recruitment:** after explicit authorization, invite a small range of clinic decision-makers and frontline staff; no outreach is authorized by this exercise. Do not request or collect patient-identifying data.
- **Interviews:** conduct separate 30-minute, non-leading interviews. Ask for a recent workflow example, role/ownership map, handoffs, exception handling, staff review, and escalation; distinguish recalled events from opinions.
- **Workflow artifact:** with permission, map stages and decision points using fictional examples only. Record current tools and accountable roles, not patient details. Verify the map with participants.
- **Pricing:** ask unaided pricing questions before presenting any price, including “zero / USD 0”; capture stated currency, amount, unit, and rationale as participant-reported, not validated willingness to pay.
- **Synthesis:** label each finding as observed artifact, participant-reported, or analyst hypothesis. Seek disconfirming cases. Compare roles and clinics without treating this synthetic brief as a baseline.
- **Decision gate:** propose a narrowly scoped, staff-supervised pilot only if ownership, workflow fit, review controls, escalation route, privacy boundaries, and a measurable non-clinical operational question are agreed. Otherwise revise or stop the hypothesis.

## Guardrails / 边界

No patient data, clinical advice, autonomous clinical decisions, promised outcomes, validated demand, real-world metrics, or claim of buyer validation. The four supplied metrics remain synthetic throughout. This document specifies a plan; it does not report completed research.
