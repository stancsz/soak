\begin{abstract}
Soak repeatedly invokes an adapter that evaluates an agent harness and model over a bounded period. It records outcomes and summaries, runs iterations sequentially in fresh adapter processes, and supports duration, timeout, interval, drain, and acceptance-gate settings. Its bundled synthetic adapter does not exercise a real model. One clinic trial involving inspection of 18 rendered pages appears in the project history, but this is not an endurance or reliability study. We describe execution semantics and propose a protocol separating smoke, endurance, recovery, and persistent-session evaluations. A passing run means only that the configured adapter reported success for completed iterations.
\end{abstract}
\section{Motivation and execution}
Short checks can miss failures that emerge during repeated use, such as latency drift, intermittent errors, or state accumulation. Soak records repeated outcomes under a bounded schedule \cite{r1}, but its result is only as meaningful as the adapter and workload.\par
Each iteration launches a fresh adapter process and iterations run sequentially. Persistent-session or memory-leak testing requires the adapter to connect to a separately managed persistent harness. Duration controls scheduling; timeouts and drain behavior bound iteration completion.\par
\section{Evidence and limitations}
The bundled adapter is synthetic. An OpenAI-compatible example checks its configured marker but does not by itself establish meaningful model or agent quality. One clinic trial involved inspection of 18 rendered pages; this is one bounded exercise and not multi-hour soak evidence.\par
Passing means the configured adapter reported success for completed iterations. It does not prove the adapter exercised the intended model path, reliability under persistent load, or that unseen failures are rare.\par
\section{Evaluation plan and conclusion}
Separate smoke checks, fixed-duration repeated workloads, persistent-session studies, and fault-injection studies. Define exposure and stopping conditions before a run. Report scheduled, started, completed, failed, timed-out, and drained iterations; retain hashes and sanitized configuration.\par
Soak provides bounded adapter-driven repetition and event accounting. Reliability conclusions require a real persistent system, sufficient exposure, and preserved failure evidence.\par
